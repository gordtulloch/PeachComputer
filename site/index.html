Build checklist — 10 phases

Building Peach 2026

A Debian-based Linux distribution that carries the Apple I through IIGS line forward — booting into any machine profile via PeachSquared, our fork of the open-source GSSquared core, with the beeps, the phosphor green, and the disk-drive chatter intact. Peach 2026 ships entirely as a VirtualBox appliance — no bare-metal install, no dual-boot. This is the phase-by-phase punch list from legal footing to launch day.
